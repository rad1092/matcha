# Bundled homebrew cartridges

The web player ships four open-source Game Boy homebrew games so it has
something to play the moment it opens. Each is redistributed unmodified
under its own license; all credit belongs to the authors below.

| ROM | Game | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tobu.gb` | Tobu Tobu Girl | Tangram Games | Code: MIT. Art and sound: CC BY 4.0 | https://github.com/SimonLarsen/tobutobugirl |
| `libbet.gb` | Libbet and the Magic Floor | Damian Yerrick (port of Martin Korth's *Magic Floor*) | zlib | https://github.com/pinobatch/libbet |
| `shocklobster.gb` | Shock Lobster | Dave VanEe | zlib | https://github.com/tbsp/shock-lobster |
| `2048.gb` | 2048-gb | Sanqui (tiles by beware & GOMF; original game by Gabriele Cirulli) | zlib | https://github.com/Sanqui/2048-gb |

The ROM images were taken from the gbdev Homebrew Hub database
(https://github.com/gbdev/database), and each license was checked against
the game's own repository.
